The current stack has a real observability mismatch:

- `controller:status` marks several residents as `STUCK`
- same-window smoke and fresh logs show at least some of those residents are
  still acting or speaking successfully

  evidence and action logs continue to advance

- `res:hans` is a false-positive `STUCK` label in `controller:status`

  effect timeout quality, not inactivity
