# Lumbridge — World Geography & Playbook

Agent-facing knowledge for navigating **Lumbridge** in the 2006 RuneJS world (revision 435).

Lumbridge is the starting area, default respawn point, and principal human-debugging anchor.

## 2. Local Amenities & Shops

### General Store (Shopkeeper & Assistant)
- **Inventory**: Tinderbox (1gp), Pot (1gp), Jug (1gp), Shears (1gp), Bucket (2gp), Bowl (4gp), Hammer (1gp), Spade (3gp).
- **Strategy**: Go here if you lose your tinderbox or need starting pots/buckets for cooking.

### Bob's Axes (Bob)
- **Inventory**: Bronze axe (16gp), Iron axe (56gp), Steel axe (200gp), Mithril axe (640gp).
- **Strategy**: Buy iron/steel woodcutting hatchets here once you reach levels 1 and 6 Woodcutting. Bob also repairs damaged barrows equipment (higher level).

### Church Altar (Father Aerec)
- **Action**: Right-click Altar → `pray-at`. Instantly restores Prayer points to max.
- **Rules**: Can be done infinitely. No GP cost.

## 3. Best Local Training Spots

### Woodcutting
- **Trees (Level 1)**: Plentiful inside castle courtyard, near the graveyard, and around the general store.
- **Oak Trees (Level 15)**: Three oaks directly west of the general store; several oaks south-west near Bob's Axes.
- **Willow Trees (Level 30)**: Along the River Lum bank, south of the bridge. Note: watch out for river-spawned water wizards if combat level is low.

### Fishing
- **River Lum (Net/Bait)**: East of the church graveyard.
  - **Net Spots**: Catch **raw shrimp** (level 1) and **raw anchovies** (level 15).
  - **Bait Spots**: Catch **raw sardine** (level 5) and **raw herring** (level 10). Requires fishing bait and a fishing rod.

### Cooking
- **Lumbridge Castle Range**: Located in the kitchen on the ground floor. 
  - **Premium Benefit**: This range has a special cooking bonus that reduces the burn rate of fish and meat compared to other standard fires and stoves. Always walk here to cook raw food gathered from the river.

### Combat & Prayer
- **Chickens (Level 1 - 3 HP)**: Pen located north-east across the bridge. Excellent for level 1 Attack/Strength/Defence. Drops **feathers** (fletching) and **raw chicken** (cooking).
- **Cows (Level 2 - 8 HP)**: Pasture north-east across the bridge (east of chickens) or pasture far north-west (behind windmill). Drops **cowhide** (save for crafting) and **raw beef**.
- **Goblins (Level 2-5)**: Scattered east of the bridge and south in the Lumbridge Swamp. Great for starter weapon upgrades (bronze spears/daggers).
- **Burying Bones**: Graveyard south of the church is a safe place to bury bones collected from training.

## 4. Travel & Routing

- **To Varrock (North)**: Follow the road north past the windmill and the sheep pen. Follow it straight north past the Champions' Guild to enter Varrock.
- **To Al-Kharid (East)**: Cross the toll gate east of the river. Requires 10gp in inventory to pass, unless you have completed the *Prince Ali Rescue* quest.
- **To Draynor Village (West)**: Exit the castle gates, walk west past the general store, and follow the path west through the forest.
- **Teleportation**: Use the **Home Teleport** spell (cooldown 30 mins) to return safely to the Lumbridge spawn point if completely lost or stuck.
