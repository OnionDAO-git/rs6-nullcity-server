# Falador — World Geography & Playbook

Agent-facing knowledge for navigating **Falador** in the 2006 RuneJS world (revision 435).

Falador is the majestic, white-walled city of the White Knights, serving as the capital of Asgarnia. It is a premier location for mining, smithing, and mid-tier shield/armour procurement.

## 2. Local Amenities & Shops

### Wayne's Chains (Wayne)
- **Location**: `2947, 3385, 0` (South of West Bank).
- **Inventory**: Bronze, Iron, and Steel chainbodies.
- **Strategy**: Go here if you need lighter torso armour with decent slash/crush defence. Wayne also has an **anvil** in his shop.

### Cassie's Shield Shop (Cassie)
- **Location**: `2972, 3386, 0` (North-west of square).
- **Inventory**: Wooden shield (20gp), Bronze square shield (48gp), Bronze kite shield (96gp), Iron square shield (168gp), Iron kite shield (336gp).
- **Strategy**: Ideal for procuring shield slot upgrades for Defence training.

### Herquin's Gems (Herquin)
- **Location**: `2946, 3336, 0` (South-west of square).
- **Inventory**: Cut sapphire, emerald, ruby.
- **Strategy**: Useful for F2P crafting or buying/selling cut gems.

## 3. Best Local Training Spots

### Mining & Dwarven Mine
- **Dwarven Mine (Entrance ladder N of Falador at X: 3018, Y: 3450)**:
  - **Copper & Tin** (Level 1) - several scattered rocks.
  - **Iron Ore** (Level 15) - 6 rocks in the southern chamber.
  - **Coal** (Level 30) - Over 10 rocks. Highly recommended for steel bars.
  - **Gold Ore** (Level 40) - 2 rocks near the central dwarven shops.
  - **Strategy**: Excellent mining spot. Mine iron or coal, climb ladder, walk south into Falador East Bank. Watch out for level 14-26 aggressive Dwarves!
- **Mining Guild (Level 60 Mining Required)**:
  - Contains **coal** and **mithril** rocks right under the city. The closest coal mine to any bank in revision 435.

### Smithing
- **smelting**: Use the **Falador Furnace** (`3027, 3353, 0`). It is located only a few tiles south of Falador East Bank, making it one of the most efficient smelting furnaces in the game.
- **Smithing Anvils**: There are no public anvils next to the furnace. Use the anvil in **Wayne's Chains** (`2947, 3385, 0`) or walk north out of the north gate to **Doric's House** (`2952, 3451, 0`) where Doric's anvil is free to use.

### Woodcutting
- **Falador Park**: Contains several normal, oak, and willow trees.
- **Yew Trees (Level 60 Woodcutting)**: Three yew trees are located just outside the south wall of Falador. Ideal for banking high-value logs at Falador East Bank.

## 4. Travel & Routing

- **To Varrock (East)**: Exit the east gate and follow the main path east past Ice Mountain. Walk past Barbarian Village and follow the road directly into Varrock.
- **To Port Sarim / Rimmington (South)**: Exit the south gate, walk south along the path to reach Port Sarim (fishing, boat trips) and Rimmington (clay mining, crafting).
- **To Taverley (West)**: Walk west out of the gate. Note that Taverley itself is a members-only gate in revision 435, but Doric's area and the mountain pass are open.
- **To Wilderness (North)**: Walk north out of the north gate past Doric's house. Walk north past the Goblin village to cross the wilderness boundary.
