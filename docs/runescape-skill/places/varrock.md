# Varrock — World Geography & Playbook

Agent-facing knowledge for navigating **Varrock** in the 2006 RuneJS world (revision 435).

Varrock is the grand capital of Misthalin. It serves as the primary trade, gear upgrade, and magic rune hub for mid-level agents.

---

| Location | Coordinates (X, Y, Z) | Description / Notes |
|---|---|---|

## 2. Local Amenities & Shops

### Varrock Sword Shop (Flynn)
- **Location**: `3204, 3400, 0` (South of square).
- **Inventory**: Bronze scimitar (32gp), Iron scimitar (112gp), Steel scimitar (400gp), Mithril scimitar (1280gp), Adamant scimitar (4096gp).
- **Strategy**: This is the single most important shop for melee gear upgrades! Always buy **scimitars** (best speed-to-damage ratio in revision 435).

### Aubury's Rune Shop (Aubury)
- **Location**: `3253, 3401, 0` (East of square, south of East Bank).
- **Inventory**: Fire rune (4gp), Water rune (4gp), Earth rune (4gp), Air rune (4gp), Mind rune (3gp), Chaos rune (90gp), Rune Essence (4gp).
- **Special Action**: Right-click Aubury → `teleport`. He will teleport you to the **Rune Essence Mine** (essential for Runecrafting, mine essence and click the portal to return).

### Lowe's Archery Emporium (Lowe)
- **Location**: `3233, 3423, 0` (East of square).
- **Inventory**: Shortbow (50gp), Oak shortbow (100gp), Willow shortbow (250gp), Bronze arrows (1gp), Iron arrows (3gp), Steel arrows (12gp).
- **Strategy**: The default shop for Ranged supplies. Buy shortbows and arrows here.

### Horvik's Armour Shop (Horvik)
- **Location**: `3228, 3436, 0` (North-east of square).
- **Inventory**: Bronze, Iron, and Steel chainbodies, platebodies, platelegs, and shields.
- **Strategy**: Buy chainbodies or platelegs here to upgrade Defence stats.

## 3. Best Local Training Spots

### Mining
- **Varrock East Mine (X: 3286, Y: 3362)**: Located outside the east wall. Contains:
  - **Copper Ore** (level 1 Mining) - 7 rocks.
  - **Tin Ore** (level 1 Mining) - 5 rocks.
  - **Iron Ore** (level 15 Mining) - 4 rocks.
  - **Strategy**: The absolute best low-level mining site. Mine copper/tin or iron, run north-west to the Varrock Furnace, smelt them, and bank at Varrock East Bank.
- **Varrock West Mine (X: 3175, Y: 3368)**: Located far south-west of the west gate. Contains:
  - **Clay** (level 1 Mining) - 3 rocks.
  - **Tin Ore** (level 1 Mining) - 8 rocks.
  - **Iron Ore** (level 15 Mining) - 3 rocks.
  - **Silver Ore** (level 20 Mining) - 2 rocks.

### Smithing
- **Smelting**: Use the **Varrock Furnace** (`3227, 3407, 0`). Smelt copper + tin into bronze bars, or iron into iron bars.
- **Smithing Bars**: Run north to **Horvik's Anvils** (`3228, 3436, 0`) with a hammer and bars to forge platebodies, daggers, or nails.

### Woodcutting
- **Oaks & Normal Trees**: The palace gardens (north of the palace walls) contain several safe oak trees. The area outside the west gate has plentiful oak and normal trees.

### Combat
- **Palace Guards (Level 21 - 22 HP)**: Patrol the castle courtyard and gates. Excellent for testing medium-level combat performance (level 10-30 combat).
- **Barbarian Village (West of Varrock)**: Walk west out of the gate to find barbarians (level 10-17), excellent for fast XP and raw meat drops.

## 4. Travel & Routing

- **To Lumbridge (South)**: Exit the south gate and follow the main road south past the sheep pasture and the windmill directly into Lumbridge.
- **To Falador (West)**: Exit the west gate, walk past Barbarian Village, and head west across the bridge past the Ice Mountain to reach Falador.
- **To Al-Kharid (South-East)**: Walk out of the south gate, turn east, and pay the 10gp toll gate to enter Al-Kharid desert.
- **DANGER WARNING (North)**: Walking north out of Varrock leads directly across the wilderness ditch. **Do not enter the Wilderness** unless on a specific wilderness combat routing run. High-level aggressive monsters (e.g. Wilderness Goblins, Skeletons) populate the border.
