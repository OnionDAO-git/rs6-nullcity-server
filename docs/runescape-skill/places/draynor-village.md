# Draynor Village — World Geography & Playbook

Agent-facing knowledge for navigating **Draynor Village** in the 2006 RuneJS world (revision 435).

Draynor Village is a gloomy, coastal village located west of Lumbridge. It is the absolute pinnacle F2P woodcutting destination for **willow logs** and the premier hub for starting **Thieving** training.

## 2. Local Amenities & Shops

### Diango's Toy Store (Diango)
- **Location**: `3082, 3251, 0` (Draynor Square).
- **Inventory**: Chronicle book (toy book that teleports you to the Champions' Guild south of Varrock - highly recommended for quick routing!).

### Ned the Sailor (Ned)
- **Location**: `3097, 3258, 0`.
- **Service**: Ned makes **rope** (essential for Prince Ali Rescue and questing). Sell him wool (4 wool = 1 rope) or buy rope from him directly for 15gp.

---

## 3. Best Local Training Spots

### Woodcutting
- **Willow Trees (Level 30 Woodcutting)**: Four willow trees sit directly along the water edge, 5 tiles south of the **Draynor Bank** entrance.
  - **Strategy**: The absolute fastest F2P woodcutting experience in the game. Fill inventory with willow logs, walk 5 steps north, deposit in bank, and repeat. High volume, very fast woodcutting XP.
- **Oaks & Normal Trees**: Abundant to the west of the square.

### Thieving
- **Market Stalls (Level 1)**: Bread stalls and seed stalls in Draynor Square.
  - **Strategy**: Steal from stalls to start Thieving. Note: watch out for level 21 Market Guards!
- **Master Farmer (Level 38 Thieving)**: Wanders Draynor Square.
  - **Strategy**: Pickpocket the Master Farmer. Highly lucrative; drops valuable herb and allotment seeds for Farming. Bring food as failures inflict 3 damage.

### Fishing & Magic
- **Wizards' Tower Bridge**: Walk south of Draynor across the long wooden bridge to find Net/Bait fishing spots. Great alternative to Lumbridge river.
- **Wizards' Tower (Dungeon)**: Contains lesser demons and wizards (great safe-spotting training for Ranged and Magic).

## 4. Travel & Routing

- **To Lumbridge (East)**: Follow the path east past the jail house, walking straight east past the Lumbridge Swamp boundary directly into Lumbridge courtyard.
- **To Port Sarim (West)**: Exit Draynor Square west along the coastline road to enter Port Sarim (docks, shops).
- **To Falador (North)**: Walk north out of the square, past the Draynor Manor boundary walls, and head north-west along the path to reach Falador East Gate.
- **DANGER WARNING (Draynor Manor)**: Entering the gates of Draynor Manor (`3108, 3274, 0`) leads to a haunted ground populated by aggressive **trees** (level 7) that will swipe and hit any nearby low-level agent. **Do not enter the manor grounds** unless completing a quest.
