# Al-Kharid — World Geography & Playbook

Agent-facing knowledge for navigating **Al-Kharid** in the 2006 RuneJS world (revision 435).

Al-Kharid is a desert city located east of the River Lum. It is the F2P capital of Crafting (leather tanning and gem jewelry) and home to the famous Al-Kharid warrior training courtyard.

## 2. Local Amenities & Shops

### Ellis' Tannery (Ellis)
- **Location**: `3275, 3191, 0` (North-east of bank).
- **Service**: Tan cowhides into Soft Leather (1gp each) or Hard Leather (3gp each).
- **Strategy**: Crucial for starting Crafting training! Collect cowhides from Lumbridge pasture, tan them here, and use needle & thread to sew leather cowls, leather bodies, or hard leather bodies.

### Dommik's Crafting Store (Dommik)
- **Location**: `3313, 3180, 0` (East of palace).
- **Inventory**: Chisel (1gp), Ring mold (5gp), Necklace mold (5gp), Amulet mold (5gp), Needle (1gp), Thread (1gp).
- **Strategy**: Buy molds for smelting silver/gold jewelry and needles/thread for sewing leather.

### Al-Kharid Kebab Shop (Karim)
- **Location**: `3300, 3180, 0`.
- **Inventory**: Kebabs (1gp).
- **Strategy**: The cheapest food source in Misthalin! Karim sells kebabs for 1gp each. 
  - **Kebab Effect**: Eating a kebab has a random effect: it can heal from 0 to 30 HP, lower stats, or boost stats. Best used as a budget food source when out of fish.

## 3. Best Local Training Spots

### Crafting & Smithing
- **Jewelry Crafting**: Smelt gold bars at the **Al-Kharid Furnace** (`3275, 3186, 0`) with gem molds (ring/necklace/amulet) to make high-value jewelry for sale or enchantment. Very close to Al-Kharid Bank.

### Combat
- **Al-Kharid Warriors (Level 9 - 19 HP)**: Over 10 warriors patrol the palace courtyard (`3293, 3179, 0`).
  - **Strategy (Warning)**: These warriors are non-aggressive, but if you attack one, **all nearby warriors will auto-retaliate and attack you at once**! This allows for high-intensity, automated combat training, but ensure you have high Defence (Steel+) and plenty of food (trout or salmon) before starting.

### Mining
- **Al-Kharid Mine (X: 3298, Y: 3298)**:
  - Contains **Copper**, **Tin**, **Iron** (3 rocks), **Coal** (3 rocks), **Silver** (5 rocks), and **Gold** (2 rocks).
  - **DANGER**: Home to level 21 aggressive **Scorpions**. Scorpions will attack lower-level mining agents automatically. Bring food and high Defence.

## 4. Travel & Routing

- **To Lumbridge (West)**: Follow the road west past the palace to the toll gate. You must pay 10gp to cross the gate into Lumbridge, unless you have completed the *Prince Ali Rescue* quest.
- **To Varrock (North)**: Follow the road north past the Al-Kharid Mine and the Duel Arena, continuing straight north through the mountain pass into Varrock.
- **To Shantay Pass / Desert (South)**: Follow the road south to Shantay Pass. The desert is extremely dangerous, requiring Shantay passes, desert clothing, and waterskins to prevent dehydration damage. **Do not enter the desert** during early runs.
