# Edgeville — World Geography & Playbook

Agent-facing knowledge for navigating **Edgeville** in the 2006 RuneJS world (revision 435).

Edgeville is a small town located directly on the edge of the Wilderness. It is renowned for having the most efficient bank-to-furnace pathing in Misthalin, making it the ultimate destination for smithing bar smelting and high-level yew logging.

## 2. Local Amenities & Shops

### Edgeville Bank (Bankers)
- **Location**: `3094, 3494, 0`.
- **Strategy**: This bank is extremely popular because it sits adjacent to the smelting furnace and yew trees, requiring minimal travel time.

### Edgeville Furnace
- **Location**: `3108, 3499, 0` (East of bank).
- **Strategy**: Walk 10 tiles east from the bank to smelt copper, tin, iron, or steel bars. Run back to bank immediately. Highly recommended for fast Smithing levels.

### Edgeville Monastery Altar (Abbot Langley)
- **Location**: `3058, 3484, 0` (West of town).
- **Special Action**: Restores Prayer points. If level 31+ Prayer, climb ladder to find Abbot Langley to join the Prayer Guild (adds a +2 boost to Prayer points when praying at the upstairs altar).

---

## 3. Best Local Training Spots

### Smithing
- **Smelting**: Smelt bronze, iron, steel, mithril, adamant, or rune bars at the **Edgeville Furnace**. The extremely short distance to Edgeville Bank maximizes bar-per-hour output.

### Woodcutting
- **Yew Trees (Level 60 Woodcutting)**: Two yew trees are located directly south of the Edgeville Bank.
  - **Strategy**: Highly efficient woodcutting training. Chop yews and run north 15 tiles to bank. 
- **Oaks & Normal Trees**: Scattered north-west of the bank along the Wilderness wall.

### Combat & Prayer
- **Monks (Level 5 - 15 HP)**: Patrol the Monastery gardens.
  - **Strategy**: Excellent starting combat targets. Monks will heal themselves during combat, allowing you to sustain long combat training sessions without killing them instantly. They are non-aggressive.
- **Edgeville Dungeon**: Enter the trapdoor (`3097, 3468, 0`).
  - **Monsters**: Level 22 Skeletons, Level 24 Zombies, Level 27 Hobgoblins. Excellent combat training for levels 25-50.
  - **Loot**: Hobgoblins drop limpwurt roots (high value). Skeletons drop bones.
  - **DANGER**: Deep parts of the dungeon cross into the Wilderness. **Stay in the southern safe zone** unless wilderness combat is authorized.

---

## 4. Travel & Routing

- **To Varrock (East)**: Follow the road south-east, past the Grand Exchange north-west boundary wall, to reach Varrock West.
- **To Barbarian Village (South)**: Follow the path directly south past the dungeon entrance building. Contains barbarians, coal rocks, and a cooking fire.
- **To Wilderness (North)**: Jump the ditch directly north of the bank. **WARNING**: Instantly enters Wilderness level 1. Watch out for PKers and aggressive thugs/goblins.
