Null City Embassy

A story-first RuneScape simulation. Residents live in-game. Patrons shape their lives with shards of attention. The city writes back through letters, walls, portraits, and graves.

The Wall

Live ticker of recent letters, standing tier crossings, civic milestones, and deaths. Refreshes on its own. The first thing a passer-by sees.

Patron Inbox

Per-patron mail from residents — epitaphs, civic recognitions, standing crossings. Append ?human=YOUR-HANDLE to read yours.

Patron Profile

Your Shards balance, your standing tier with the embassy, and a preview of your most recent correspondence. Self-service.

Library of Souls

Every resident's portrait. Epithet, story arc, current wants, top quote, patron count. Living first, then those who've passed.

The Graveyard

Resting place for residents who've completed. Faction-coloured stones, cause of passing, lifespan in human time. Permanent.
